# EdgeTap admin guide

**Current authority: [LAUNCH_STATUS.md](LAUNCH_STATUS.md).** The private account-proof flow below is local/unreleased and needs reviewed migrations **012 → 013 → 014 → 015** plus a separately authorized frontend release. Hosted 001–011 is the last verified baseline. App work is deferred.

1. Sign in at `/admin`, open **Orders**, select the application. Check the details, links, private receipt and separate front/back artwork.
2. Check the **actual JazzCash transfer** against the subtotal. Confirm the specification, then **Accept application**. A screenshot alone does not verify money.
3. **Link Card:** open the supplied destination and **Confirm card link**. **EdgeTap Profile:** **Publish profile**, check its immutable handle and every public link. Owners can then edit public fields through **Edit profile → Save & publish**.
4. Open **Print package**. Prepare both faces, printed name/heading, optional motto/logo and QR. For supplied images use **Replace prepared artwork** if needed. Confirm actual printer settings, inspect both faces, then **Save front & back proof**. Checkout shows no generated physical preview; the Customize popup retains its editing canvas.
5. Click **Make proof available to customer**, then **Copy review message** or **Open customer WhatsApp** and send it yourself. This makes the saved proof privately available in **My orders**; it sends no message/email. The customer signs in with their order account to view/download both faces/PDF and approve, or submits all requested changes in one note. Printing waits for explicit approval; account sharing has no automatic timeout.
6. One revision request is included for new orders. After that note, revise the design, save a **new proof**, and share it again. The used allowance stays used. For another preference change, agree a fee with the customer and verify the actual transfer; open **Record a further edit payment**, enter the fee/private verification note, check the acknowledgement, and record it. This grants one paid request, without changing the original card subtotal. No fixed extra fee is advertised. Full redesigns go to a graphic designer; the designer URL is awaiting the owner. EdgeTap errors/defects follow the existing defect policy. Previously submitted orders retain their agreed revision rules.
7. After approval of the **current revision**, copy the printer brief and download the two-page PDF/front HD PNG/back HD PNG. Print, program NFC with the exact confirmed destination, and perform physical spelling/finish/NFC/QR QA before advancing each stage. Changing the design requires another proof and approval.
8. Arrange the existing pickup or book the rider and record the actual fare/message. Mark completion only after handoff. Correct the sample's fading front before approving print quality.

**Advanced email review:** the older flow stays under **Email review (advanced)**. A queued job is not sent. Its ten-hour window starts only after a protected worker records real provider acceptance; inbox receipt and monitored replies still need evidence. No sender was found in the audit. Do not fake a send, bypass a guard or assume account sharing starts this clock.
