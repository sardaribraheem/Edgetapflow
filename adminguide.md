# EdgeTap admin guide

1. Sign in at `/admin` with your verified staff account. Open **Orders** and select the application.
2. Check the customer's details, links, private receipt and artwork. Send a message to request corrections if needed.
3. Verify the **actual JazzCash transfer** against the card subtotal. Confirm the final card specification/artwork, then **Accept application**.
4. **Publish profile**: check the handle and create it. Open the public page and test every link. The customer can sign in, click **Edit profile**, and **Save & publish** their name, photo, bio and links. Handles stay fixed.
5. Send the real design-proof email and wait for the confirmed review. A queued email is not sent. The 10-hour window starts after confirmed sending; printing stays held until review finishes.
6. Complete actual production: printing → NFC programmed with the canonical profile URL → QR/NFC and spelling QA. Update each stage only after doing the work.
7. Arrange NASTP pickup, or book the rider and record the actual fare/message. The customer pays delivery to the rider. Mark received/completed after the handoff.
